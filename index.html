Why a kitchen with no tables

Sriyan started as weekend cooking for hostel friends who missed home food. Once the orders outgrew one stove, we moved into a proper kitchen — but kept the part that mattered: everything made after you order, not before.

There's no dining room because there doesn't need to be one. The kitchen's whole budget goes into ingredients, oil that gets changed on schedule, and getting your food to you while it's still hot — not into rent for a room you'd eat in for twenty minutes.
